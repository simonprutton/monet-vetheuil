Seen from the riverbank in the cool grey light of late autumn, the twelfth-century church of Notre-Dame de Vétheuil rises above its reflection in the slow-moving Seine. Monet returned to this subject many times, drawn by the interplay of stone and water. This Orsay canvas was painted in a year of great personal difficulty, while Monet nursed his dying wife Camille, and the subdued palette carries an unmistakable weight of sorrow.
