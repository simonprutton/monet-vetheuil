A dusty track winds through the outskirts of Vétheuil in the golden light of early autumn, the foliage just beginning to turn. Painted during Monet's first full year in the village, the composition retains the confident brushwork of his Argenteuil period while adopting a more solitary, introspective mood. The Phillips Collection's acquisition of this work reflects its status as a quiet gem of the Vétheuil years.
