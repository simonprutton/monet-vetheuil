Twenty years after leaving Vétheuil, Monet returned to paint the village from the opposite bank of the Seine at Lavacourt. The architectural forms of the church and houses are almost dissolved in shimmering light and colour, the water surface broken into thousands of individual brushstrokes. Compared with the more descriptive paintings of the 1870s, this late revisitation reveals how profoundly Monet's vision had shifted towards pure sensation.
