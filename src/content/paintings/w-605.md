Painted from a boat on the Seine in high summer, this panoramic canvas shows the village of Vétheuil cascading down the hillside towards the river, with the church of Notre-Dame anchoring the skyline. The intense blue water and hazy golden light capture the atmosphere of a hot August afternoon. It is one of Monet's most celebrated images of the village and a cornerstone of the Metropolitan Museum's Impressionist collection.
