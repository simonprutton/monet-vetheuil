Sunflowers, dahlias, and climbing nasturtiums fill this exuberant composition, painted in the final summer Monet spent at Vétheuil before moving to Poissy. Two of Alice Hoschedé's children can be glimpsed ascending the steep garden steps, lending a sense of domestic warmth. The painting's lush verticality and saturated colour anticipate the great garden canvases Monet would create at Giverny throughout the following decades.
